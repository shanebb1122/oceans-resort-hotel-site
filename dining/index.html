Te Ākau Roa

Restaurant & Bar · Tūtūkākā Marina

Manaakitanga at the Marina

Welcome to Te Ākau Roa Restaurant & Bar — our in-house restaurant where manaakitanga and the spirit of te ao Māori infuse every dining experience. Guided by our very own local Head Chef, we celebrate Northland with dishes inspired by kai moana and the bounty of the land, crafted to honour Ngāti Wai's deep connection to place.

Overlooking the Tūtūkākā Marina, Te Ākau Roa is where culture, flavour, and world-class skill come together to create a taste of Northland you'll never forget.

Planning a coastal stay? Explore our Tūtūkākā rooms and apartments. For a group visit, see our conference and event facilities or enquire about dining at Te Ākau Roa.

Our Menus

All Day Brunch

Available every day from 8:00am until 3:00pm. Start your morning with a hearty coastal breakfast, fresh barista coffee, and marina views — or settle in for a relaxed midday brunch before your afternoon adventures.

Dinner

Available Thursday through Saturday evenings from 5:00pm until 8:30pm. A refined dining experience celebrating kai moana and the finest Northland produce — locally sourced seafood, premium cuts, and a curated selection of New Zealand wines.

Drinks

Enjoy New Zealand wines, craft beers, cocktails, coffee, and more at our bar overlooking the marina. Our full drinks list covers everything from barista coffee to spirits and smoothies.

Vegetarian

A dedicated vegetarian dinner menu celebrating fresh, seasonal produce. From garlic focaccia and mushroom arancini to kumara gnocchi and dark chocolate tart — full flavour, no compromise.

Kids

Little ones are well catered for with a dedicated kids menu — sloppy joes, crispy fish bites, grilled chicken, bangers and mash, and ice cream sundaes to finish.
