Conferences & Events in Tūtūkākā

Explore accommodation for your group and Te Ākau Roa Restaurant & Bar as you plan your meeting or event.

A Coastal Setting for Serious Work

Quality Hotel Oceans Tūtūkaka sits right on the marina, just 30 minutes from Whangārei CBD and under three hours from Auckland. We offer two versatile conference spaces, on-site accommodation, and tailor-made catering from our in-house restaurant, Te Ākau Roa.

Our super king rooms can be reconfigured into twin kings for delegates. With free parking, a pool, BBQ area, and a range of local activities, we make it easy to blend productive sessions with memorable team experiences.

Activities & Leisure

Make the most of the Tūtūkaka Coast between sessions. Our team can help plan the perfect group itinerary.

Dive & Snorkel

Explore the world-renowned Poor Knights Islands Marine Reserve — home to the world's largest sea cave — with Dive Tutukaka.

Deep-Sea Fishing

Head out for big-game fishing with Fish Tutukaka, one of the region's premier charter operators.

Paddle & Surf

Paddle board, sea kayak, or surf along the stunning Tutukaka Coast with Pacific Coast Kayaks or Tutukaka Surf.

Golf

Tee off at Ngunguru Golf Course, Northland Golf Course, or the world-class Kauri Cliffs at Matauri Bay.

Coastal Walks

Walk to the Tūtūkaka Lighthouse, Whale Bay, or the Ngunguru Estuary — all stunning and accessible straight from the hotel.

Tennis

Hit the courts at one of the local tennis facilities nearby — a great option for a quick team break between sessions.

Fitness & Wellness

CoreFitness gym is in the hotel complex and offers Les Mills classes daily — or arrange a private group session as a team activity. Features free weights, yoga mats, and open floor space. Extra charges apply.
