Where the Pacific Meets Refined Comfort

Set beside the stunning Tūtūkākā Harbour with views stretching out to the Poor Knights Islands Marine Reserve, Quality Hotel Oceans Tūtūkaka is a destination designed for rest and rejuvenation.

Whether you're here to dive the world-famous marine reserve, explore pristine coastal beaches, or simply watch the boats gently bob in the marina from our sun-warmed deck, you'll find a genuine New Zealand welcome waiting for you.
