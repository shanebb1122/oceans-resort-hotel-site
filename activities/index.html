Tūtūkākā Coast Activities

Your gateway to the Tūtūkākā Coast — world-class diving, surfing, fishing, and adventure, all on our doorstep.

Explore rooms and apartments at Tūtūkākā Marina and dining at Te Ākau Roa as you plan your coastal stay. Contact us about accommodation.

Poor Knights Islands Marine Reserve

Half an hour from Whangārei, Tūtūkākā is the gateway to the Poor Knights Islands Marine Reserve — rated one of the top ten dive sites in the world, with crystal-clear waters and an incredible abundance of sea life. The reserve is also home to the world's largest sea cave, an astonishing 7.9 million cubic feet in volume.

The islands carry a rich cultural history as land of Ngātiwai. Since 1822 they have been tapu (sacred), with no one permitted ashore — preserving them as one of New Zealand's most special nature reserves. Visit Tūtūkaka Coast.com for more information.

See the Poor Knights

Take a closer look at the marine reserve that makes the Tūtūkākā Coast one of the world's great diving destinations.

Perfect Day

A very special day trip, Perfect Day offers the ultimate non-diving Poor Knights Islands experience — sight-seeing, kayaking, cave exploration, snorkelling, swimming, sea mammal spotting, fascinating island history, and great food. The boat is beautifully fitted out with plenty of indoor and outdoor seating, upstairs viewing, and a live underwater "fish cam" feeding footage straight into the boat.

Tūtūkaka Surf Co.

Tūtūkaka Surf Company will introduce you to this awesome part of New Zealand and help you experience the magic of surf riding, in a safe environment with qualified coaches. Their shop is located in the same building as Oceans Resort Hotel, catering for all your surfing, clothing, and home retail needs — whether you're learning to surf, reigniting a love of the water, or bringing your surfing passion to the Tūtūkaka Coast.

Dive Now

Tūtūkākā's dedicated dive operator, based right at the marina. Dive Now runs daily dive trips to the Poor Knights Islands Marine Reserve, offering introductory dives, guided dives, and PADI certification courses — all with quality equipment hire available. Whether you're a first-timer or a seasoned diver, their expert crew make every trip to one of the world's top dive sites truly memorable.

As a hotel guest, ask at reception about exclusive Stay & Dive packages — we're working closely with Dive Now to bring you the best possible experience on the water.

Fish Tūtūkaka

Create your own ocean adventure — a fully personalised trip snorkelling and exploring the Poor Knights, stand-up paddleboarding along the Tūtūkaka Coast, or fishing from Bream Bay to Cape Brett, including deep water and big game fishing. Combo fish-and-explore trips are perfect for the whole family, or make it your next work function — our local operator has it all covered.

Gym & Fitness Studio

Core Fitness in the Oceans complex is available to hotel guests for a nominal charge. Ask at reception about the group fitness classes on offer — the gym also features weights machines, floor space, yoga mats, and free weights.

We can also arrange a private class for your group as a team-building activity — perhaps even out on the village green.
